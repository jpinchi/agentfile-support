About the App

Agent File is an FBI career preparation app featuring daily AI-powered investigations, real federal case studies, interview prep, and a live federal job board. Powered by Claude AI (Anthropic).

Frequently Asked Questions

  • What is an Anthropic API key? It's a free key from console.anthropic.com that powers the AI features.
  • Is my API key stored securely? Yes — it's stored locally on your device using iOS Keychain.
  • Why does the daily case reset at midnight? A new AI-generated case is created every 24 hours.
  • The app is not loading new cases. Check your internet connection and that your API key is valid.
  • How do I delete my data? Go to Settings → scroll to the bottom → Delete Account.
